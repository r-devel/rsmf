Project Overview

Background

The Research Software Maintenance Fund supports projects that improve sustainability, reduce technical debt, and enhance community engagement.



Our RSMF-funded project, Enabling the Next Generation of Contributors to R, aims to put the R project on a more sustainable footing.

Aims

  1. Build collaboration between R Core developers and advanced contributors

  2. Support broader engagement in R contribution.

  3. Improve communication between R Core and the wider community.

  4. Improve the sustainability of the R Foundation
